              94% caught
            
    
            
    
              
    HookLine
    
              
    A phishing email analyser that runs 13 red flag checks, from SPF, DKIM and DMARC failures to brand names disguised with invisible characters, then checks attachments, links and domains on VirusTotal and explains its Safe, Suspicious or Malicious verdict. It runs from the terminal, a Flask web interface or a reporting inbox over IMAP. Tested on real phishing emails from a public honeypot dataset, it caught 47 of 50 (94%), and all 30 safe test emails passed.
    
              
    I built HookLine to understand how phishing emails get past people, and to check each email by its evidence, not by gut feeling.

  1. 
            [01]
            
    Spec it first
    
            
    I wrote a 35 requirement spec for Spybot and built every one. Knowing what "done" means keeps a project honest.
    
          
    2. 
          
  2. 
            [02]
            
    Test as I build

    3. 
          
  3.
